A few things worth knowing.

Voting, payments, and what happens to your slides afterwards.

Who can vote?

Anyone signed in with Google who has finished their profile. One vote per deck, and you can't vote for your own team. If the organiser created categories, you get one vote in each.

Can I see the vote counts while voting is open?

No. Counts stay hidden until the organiser closes voting. It keeps the race honest and stops early leaders from snowballing.

How do I pay the entry fee?

Open the event poster, scan the UPI QR on it, and pay from your own UPI app. Then paste the reference number into the registration form so the organiser can confirm it. Money never passes through OLTA.

What files can I upload?

PDF or PPTX, up to 25 MB, and up to 15 slides unless the organiser sets a different limit. PDF is the safer choice — your fonts and layout stay exactly as you designed them.

Can people download my deck?

Not by default. Your slides are shown as images with your team name on them. The organiser can turn downloads on for an event, and you'll see it on the upload screen if they have.

What happens to my deck after the event?

It stays public for 30 days, then only you and the organiser can see it. Original files are deleted after 90 days. Winning decks stay on the results page. You can delete yours at any time.

How do I become an organiser?

Organiser access comes from an invite link sent by whoever runs the fest. Sign in first, then open the link — your account switches to organiser and you can create the event.
