A few things worth knowing.

Voting, money, and what happens to your slides later.

Who can vote?

Anyone signed in with Google who filled in their profile. One vote per deck, and you cannot vote for your own team. If the organiser made groups, you get one vote in each group.

Can I see the counts while voting is open?

No. Counts stay hidden until the organiser ends voting. That keeps the race fair and stops an early leader from running away with it.

How do I enter the contest?

Sign up on the poster. The poster carries the organiser's Google Form and payment QR, and the organiser checks the proof there. Podium starts after that: you come here with the event code, upload your deck, and the room votes.

What files can I upload?

PDF or PPTX, up to 25 MB, and up to 15 slides unless the organiser sets another limit. PDF is safer: your fonts and layout stay exactly as you made them.

Can people download my deck?

Not unless the organiser allows it. Your slides show up as pictures with your team name on them. If downloads are on, you will see it on the upload screen.

What happens to my deck after the event?

It stays open to everyone for 30 days. After that, only you and the organiser can see it. We delete the file itself after 90 days. Winning decks stay on the results page. You can delete yours any time.

How do I become an organiser?

Someone running the fest sends you an invite link. Sign in first, then open the link. Your account turns into an organiser account and you can make the event.
